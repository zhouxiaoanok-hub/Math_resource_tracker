   ## Paper / 论文
- [Paper] Formalization of De Giorgi–Nash–Moser Theory in Lean — https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05984 — Armstrong & Kempe's ~56,000-line, sorry-free Lean 4 formalization of elliptic regularity theory (local boundedness, weak Harnack, Hölder regularity) is directly relevant to geometric analysis and math.AP formalization; code at https://github.com/scottnarmstrong/DeGiorgi.
- [Paper] LeanGeo: Formalizing Competitional Geometry problems in Lean — https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14644 — introduces a Lean 4 theorem library and the LeanGeo-Bench benchmark (IMO-level problems) for synthetic/competition geometry, integrated with Mathlib.
- [Paper] Euclean: Automated Geometry Problem Formalization with Unified Verification in Lean — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19374 — automated pipeline for formalizing Euclidean geometry problems in Lean with unified verification.
- [Paper] MechGeo: Autoformalizing and Proving Euclidean Geometry in Lean 4 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02295 — autoformalization + proof search system targeting Euclidean geometry in Lean 4.
